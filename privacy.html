MEDIAX Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

MEDIAX helps users identify supported media platforms and inspect direct media file links. This policy explains how information is handled.

2. Information We Process

When you submit a link, our service processes that link to identify the platform or inspect supported media file formats.

Do not submit passwords, private account links, personal information, or confidential content.

3. Browser Storage

MEDIAX may store recent links in your browser's local storage to show your local history. This information is stored on your device and may remain until you clear it or clear your browser data.

4. Third-Party Platforms

Links to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and other services are subject to those platforms' own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the service. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

MEDIAX is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should not submit personal or sensitive information.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information published on our Contact page.
